# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The intended visitor explores the experience privately, at an unhurried pace, on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

Present six personal reasons as an intimate underwater discovery. Success means the invitation feels thoughtful, playful, sincere, and easy to explore without pressure.

## Brand Personality

Contemplative, warm, and playfully intimate.

## Anti-references

- Professional portfolio or resume presentation.
- Excessively dramatic declarations or emotional pressure.
- Bright tropical-aqua screens, generic glass-card grids, or loud animation.

## Design Principles

- Let discovery do more work than explanation.
- Keep the ocean present throughout every interaction.
- Make romance feel personal and light, never performative.
- Treat motion as atmosphere and always preserve stillness as an option.
- Keep personal content centralized and simple to replace.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard behavior. Support reduced motion, clear focus, semantic links, an accessible dialog, comfortable touch targets, and content that remains complete without hover.
